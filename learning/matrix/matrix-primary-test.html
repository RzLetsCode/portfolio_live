Matrix Properties & Fundamentals

A comprehensive guide to matrix theory, linear transformations, and interactive self-assessment.

1. Matrix Core Definitions

A matrix is a rectangular grid of numbers arranged in rows and columns. If a vector is a single list of numbers, a matrix is a table of them.

$$A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 4 & 5 & 6 \end{bmatrix}$$

You can also think of a matrix as a stack of vectors. Each entry is designated by $A_{ij}$ for row $i$, column $j$.

2. Transpose & Trace

The transpose $A^T$ flips $A$ along its diagonal, turning rows into columns. For an $m \times n$ matrix $A$, $A^T$ is $n \times m$.

The trace $\text{tr}(A)$ is the sum of main diagonal elements for a square matrix and equals the sum of its eigenvalues.

3. Rank & Vector Subspaces

The rank measures linearly independent rows or columns.

Rank-Nullity Theorem: $$\text{rank}(A) + \text{nullity}(A) = \text{number of columns of } A$$
