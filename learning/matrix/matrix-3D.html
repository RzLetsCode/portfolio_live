Interactive 3D Matrix Transformations

Rotate, zoom, and adjust parameters to visualize how matrices transform space, scale areas, and define surfaces.

1. Determinant (Area Transformation in 2D/3D)

A matrix transforms the standard unit square (or cube) into a parallelogram. The determinant represents the area scale factor.

Determinant: 3.00

2. Rank & Column Space Collapse

When columns are linearly independent, the column space spans a full plane (Rank 2). When columns are linearly dependent, the matrix squashes space down to a single line (Rank 1).

Rank: 2 (Plane)

3. Positive Definite Quadratic Surfaces

For a positive definite matrix $A$, the quadratic form $z = \mathbf{x}^T A \mathbf{x}$ forms a bowl with a unique minimum at zero.

Status: Positive Definite
